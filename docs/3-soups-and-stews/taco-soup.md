# Taco Soup   

## Ingredients:

* 2 lbs ground beef, drained  
* 1 large onion  
* 1 pkg taco seasoning  
* 1/2 tsp chili powder  
* 1 pkg hidden valley salad mix  
* 1 can chopped green chilis 4.5 oz  
* 1 can ranch style pinto beans  
* 1 can ranch style with jalapenos  
* 1 can whole kernel corn  
* 1 can rotel  
* 2 cans diced tomatoes  
* 2 cups Chicken Broth

## Directions:

1. Empty all cans, seasoning packets, and chili powder into crock pot  
2. Chop onion, and add  
3. Brown beef, drain, and add  
4. Stir well and cook on low for 5-6 hours  
5. Serve over chips with cheese, hot sauce, jalapenos, sour cream, or whatever you’d like

## Notes:

* When adding canned items, do not drain.  Add the liquid to the crock pot.
