# Chicken Riggies

## Ingredients:
- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 8oz mushrooms, sliced
- ½ cup seeded jarred cherry peppers, sliced
- 2 TBS butter
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 1 medium red bell pepper
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 1 16oz jar pasta sauce
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- 1 lb uncooked rigatoni pasta
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream

## Directions:

1. On medium heat, cook mushrooms with a little water, until all water is released
2. Add butter, onion, and red bell pepper and sweat until soft
3. Add garlic and sliced peppers, sweat until fragrant, ~45s
4. Deglaze with white wine and simmer until reduced by half, 5 mins
5. Add tomatoes, oregano, sugar, chicken, reduce heat and simmer for 45m or until a wooden spoon leaves a trail that doesnt come back to together
