# Air Fryer Hot Wings  

## Ingredients:

* Prep  
  * 2 lbs drums and flats  
  * 1.5 tsp baking powder  
  * 1.5 tsp cornstarch  
* Dry Rub  
  * 1.5 tsp kosher salt  
  * 1 tsp ground pepper  
  * 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
  * 1/2 tsp onion powder  
  * 1 tsp paprika  
  * 2 tbsp canola oil  
* Sauce  
  * ⅔ c franks red hot  
  * ½ c butter  
  * ½ tsp worcestershire sauce

## Directions:

1. Cut and trim the wings, then pat them very dry  
2. Place the drums and flats in a bowl and sprinkle the baking powder and cornstarch on top.  Toss and cover the meat completely.  
3. Place on a baking sheet and chill for 1 hour  
4. Pre-heat the air fryer to 400f  
5. Place the meat in a bowl and cover in the canola oil.  Sprinkle pre-mixed dry rub ingredients and toss, covering evenly.  
6. Space the wings evenly in the basket and air fry for 20m, turning once  
7. In a saucepan, melt the butter and add the hot sauce and worcestershire sauce  
8. Toss the wings in the sauce and serve immediately
