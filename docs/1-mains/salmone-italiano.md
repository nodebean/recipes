# Salmone Italiano  

## Ingredients:

* 4 (5 oz) salmon fillets   
* 2 teaspoons \+ 2 tablespoons olive oil  
* Salt and freshly ground pepper  
* 2 tomatoes chopped or 1 (14oz) can diced tomatoes, drained   
* 2 chopped shallots   
* 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice   
* 4-5 lemon slices (thin)   
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano   
* 1 teaspoon dried thyme  

## Directions:

1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F  
2. Sprinkly salmon with 2 teaspoons olive oil, salt, and pepper.   
3. Stir the tomatoes, shallots, 2 tablespoons of oil, lemon juice, oregano, thyme, a bit more salt and pepper in a medium bowl.   
4. On a baking sheet, place a salmon fillet, oiled side down, atop a sheet of foil (you will need enough foil to fully enclose the fillet) . Wrap the ends of the foil to form a little boat. Spoon the tomato mixture over the salmon, evenly distributing to all the fillet packs. Then place a lemon slice on top and seal up the packs by twisting the foil.   
5. Bake until the salmon is just cooked through, about 25 minute.   
6. Saluti\!
