# Cajun Pasta   

## Ingredients:

* 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil  
* 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1 inch pieces  
* 1 1/2 tsp cajun seasoning   
* 1 red bell pepper, sliced into strips about 2 inches long  
* 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced into strips about 2 inches long  
* 1 red onion, sliced  
* 3 cloves garlic, minced   
* 2 (14.5 oz) cans low sodium chicken broth  
* 1 (14.5 oz) can fire roasted diced tomatoes  
* 3/4 cup heavy cream, divided  
* 12 oz penne pasta (barilla holds the best)  
* 2 tsp cornstarch, mixed with 1 tbsp water   
* 3/4 cup finely shredded parmesan cheese   
* 3 tbsp parsley fresh (2 tbsp if using dried)   
* Salt and pepper to taste 

## Directions: 

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat (I use my enameled cast iron)   
2. Toss chicken with 1/2 tbsp cajun seasoning. Add to pot in a single layer.   
3. Cook until browned on bottom, about 3 minutes then turn and continue to cook through, about 3 minutes longer. Transfer to a sheet of foil and wrap to keep warm   
4. Heat remaining 1/2 tbsp of olive oil in same pot over on medium-high heat. Add bell peppers and onion and saute until soft (about 5 minutes). Add garlic and remaining 1 tbsp cajun seasoning and saute 1 minute longer  
5. Pour in chicken broth, tomatoes and 1/2 cup heavy cream. Bring mixture to boil and then add pasta  
6. Cook, stirring frequently until pasta is nearly tender, about 10-12 minutes. Add cornstarch and water mixture and cook about 1 minute longer until sauce has started to thicken.   
7. Remove from heat. Toss in remaining 1/4 cup cream, parmesan, parsley, and cooked chicken. Season with salt and pepper to taste.   
8. Enjoy\!
