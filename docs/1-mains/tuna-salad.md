# Tuna Salad

## Ingredients:

* 1 can tuna in water  
* 1 hard boiled egg, yolk and white separated  
* 1/4 onion finely diced  
* 3 tbs Mayonnaise  
* 1 tsp Mustard  
* 1/4c chopped Pickles  
* Salt and Pepper

## Directions:

1. Mix well the mayo mustard and egg yolk in a bowl, chop up the egg white  
2. Add the tuna onion and pickles into the mixture and mix well  
3. Add more mayo to taste
