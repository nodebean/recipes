# Basic Chicken Pot Pie  

## Ingredients:

* Rotisserie chicken  
* 1 can cream of mushroom  
* 1 can cream of chicken  
* 16oz bag frozen mixed vegetables  
* 2 frozen pie crusts  
* Puff pastry  
* 1/2 tsp rosemary  
* 1/2 tsp thyme  
* 1/2 tsp parsley  
* 1/2 tsp black pepper  
* 1/2 tsp kosher salt  
* 1 small onion  
* 1/2 tbs butter  
* 2 tbs butter, melted

## Directions:

1. Preheat oven to 425 F  
2. Small dice and sweat small onion in 1/2 tsp butter until soft  
3. Combine meat from chicken, vegetables, soup, herbs, and s/p in a bowl, mix well.   
4. In a 9x13 dish spread out the 2 pie crusts, evenly distribute the mixture on top  
5. Add the puff pastry to the top then brush with butter  
6. Bake 30-40 minutes until golden  
7. Let cool somewhat and serve
