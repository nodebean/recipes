# Southern Banana Pudding  

## Ingredients:

* 1 1/2 cups sugar  
* 2/3  cup all-purpose flour  
* 1/2  teaspoon salt  
* 6 slightly beaten egg yolks  
* 4 cups whole milk  
* 4 tablespoons butter  
* 2 teaspoons Vanilla extract  
* Bananas cut into 1/4in coins  
* Nilla Wafers

## Directions:

1. In a saucepan, combine sugar, flour and salt  
2. Gradually stir in milk. Cook and stir over medium high heat until bubbly. Cook and stir 2 minutes  
3. Stir a moderate amount of the hot mixture into the yolks then immediately return to hot mixture  
4. Cook 2 more minutes stirring constantly  
5. Allow the mixture to cool a bit.  
6. In a deep dish, layer mixture, then bananas, then the nilla wafers until mixture is used up.  
7. Allow to cool
