# Green Bean Casserole

## Ingredients:

* 2 cans (10 1/2 ounces) Cream of Mushroom Soup  
* 1 cup whole milk  
* 2 teaspoon soy sauce  
* 8 cups cooked cut green beans, drained  
* 2 2/3 cups French's® French Fried Onions  
* 16oz sliced mushrooms  
* ½ tbs butter  
* 2 shallots  
* Salt and pepper to taste

## Directions:

1. Saute mushrooms in a bit of water until evaporated  
2. Add butter and diced shallot to the pan with a pinch of salt, cook until clear  
3. In a casserole dish combine ingredients, including ⅔ cup of the dried onions  
4. Cover with the rest of the french fried onions  
5. Bake for 25 minutes at 350 until bubbly
