# Greek Lemon Potatoes

## Ingredients:

* 3 cloves garlic, minced  
* 1/3 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (from 2 medium lemons)  This is a little bright.  Consider 1 lemon  
* 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth or low-sodium vegetable broth  
* 1/2 cup olive oil  
* 2 teaspoons dried oregano  
* 1 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt  
* 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper  
* 2 pounds Yukon gold potatoes (3 large or 4 medium), peeled and cut into 1-inch-wide wedges

## Directions:

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 400℉.  
2. Place 3 minced garlic cloves, 1/3 cup lemon juice, 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth, 1/2 cup olive oil, 2 teaspoons dried oregano, 1 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Stir to combine.  
3. Add 2 pounds Yukon gold potato wedges to the baking dish and toss to coat. Spread into an even layer. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil.  
4. Roast for 30 minutes. Uncover and toss the potatoes. Return the baking dish to the oven and roast uncovered until the potatoes are very tender, 30 to 35 minutes more. If you’d like browned edges, turn the oven to broil and broil until the potatoes are lightly browned, 2 to 4 minutes.
