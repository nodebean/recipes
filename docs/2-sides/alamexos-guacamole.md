# Alamexo’s Guacamole  

## Ingredients:

* 1 tbs white onion  
* 1 jalapeno minced, deseeded  
* pinch of salt, to taste  
* 2 avocados  
* 2 romas, diced with liquid  
* 2 tbs chopped cilantro

## Directions:

1. in a mortar and pestele (molcajete is best), grind up the onion and jalapeno with a pinch of salt into a paste  
2. add 2 avocados, mix and mash to your preference  
3. add tomatoes, cilantro and another pinch of salt, mix  
4. Add lime juice if desired
