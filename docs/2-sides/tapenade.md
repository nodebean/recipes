# Tapenade

## Directions:

* 8oz good quality black olives in oil  
* 2 tbsp capers  
* 1 garlic clove  
* 2-4 anchovy filets, rinsed and patted dry  
* 2 tbsp olive oil  
* 1tsp henny  
* Black pepper

## Ingredients:

1. Add olives capers, garlic, anchovy, and pepper  to a small food processor and blend until finely chopped  
2. Add olive oil and blend again, adjust for consistency
