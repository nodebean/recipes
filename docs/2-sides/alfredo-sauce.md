# Alfredo Sauce  

## Ingredients:

* 1 pint heavy cream  
* 1/2 stick butter  
* 1 cup Parmesan cheese  
* pinch of nutmeg  
* pinch of parsley  
* salt and pepper to taste

## Directions:

1. Melt butter on low and blend in cream.   
2. Remove from heat and add Parmesan cheese, nutmeg, parsley, salt and pepper.   
3. Pour over fettuccine.
