# Salsa #2  

## Ingredients:

* 1 can 28 oz whole tomatoes  
* 2 jalapenos seeded and chopped  
* 2 serranos seeded and chopped (you can add more if you like it more spicy)  
* 2 gloves fresh garlic \- crushed  
* Half of a white onion, chopped into smaller pieces  
* Cilantro chopped to taste or \~¼ cup

## Directions:

1. Remove whole tomatoes from juice (but keep the juice) and place in blender.   
2. Pulse a couple of times until tomatoes are almost like a sauce.   
3. Add remaining ingredients.  
4. Pulse a couple of more times until desired consistency.   
5. If you want the salsa to be thinner, add more of the reserved tomato juice.  
6. Add Salt and pepper to taste.

## Notes:

* Let sit in the fridge at least over night. The longer it sits \- the better it gets.
