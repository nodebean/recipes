# Homemade Croutons  

## Ingredients:

* ½ cup olive oil  
* 4 cloves garlic, finely minced  
* 1 (8 ounce) loaf French bread baguette, cut into 1/2-inch cubes  
* ¾ cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, divided  
* ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning  
* ½ teaspoon paprika  
* ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper  
* ½ teaspoon salt  
* 1 pinch cayenne pepper

## Directions:

1. Stir olive oil and garlic together in a bowl. Let sit at room temperature until flavors are infused, about 3 hours.  
2. Preheat oven to 300 degrees. Place a silicone baking sheet on a backing pan.  
3. Place bread cubes in a large bowl. Pour garlic oil through a strainer onto the bread; toss to coat bread with oil.   
4. Add 1/2 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, Italian seasoning, paprika, black pepper, salt, and cayenne pepper; toss to coat.  
5. Spread bread cubes out in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.  
6. Bake in the preheated oven for 15 minutes.   
7. Turn croutons and bake 15 minutes more.   
8. Push croutons together with a spatula so that they are in a compact rectangle on the baking sheet and no longer spread out; sprinkle remaining 1/4 cup cheese over the top.   
9. Bake until cheese is melted and the bread is crispy and crunchy, 10 to 15 minutes more.
